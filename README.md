## 4D Invariant Matrix Cosmology

宇宙の根本原理は、驚くほどシンプルです。<br>

私たちが観測している宇宙の全現象は、この「4次元空間におけるエネルギー密度の波動伝播」という単一の現象として美しく記述できます。<br>

### 概要
本理論は、既存の一般相対性理論における「幾何学的時空の歪み」という仮定を排除し、余剰次元を必要としない静的4次元ブロック宇宙（不変母体）構造の上に、宇宙の全物理現象を統合する新たな数理物理モデルである。<br>
重力相互作用および光の軌道曲率（重力レンズ効果）は、空間自体の変形ではなく、4次元母体上のエネルギー密度 $u$ の勾配 $\nabla u$ およびそれに伴う波動の屈折現象として説明される。<br>
さらに、局所的な「波動伝播（慣性）」と巨視的な「熱的平均化（拡散）」を単一の統合方程式へ結びつけることで、エネルギー保存法則（ネーターの定理）および宇宙循環サイクルを論理的一貫性を保って記述する。<br>

### Introduction
The fundamental principle of the universe is surprisingly simple.<br>
There is no need to introduce complex concepts such as "warped spacetime," "extra dimensions," "cosmic expansion," or "dark energy."<br>
In a static 4-dimensional space, when energy density exceeds a critical threshold, fluctuations in density radiate outward infinitely as concentric ripples—much like stones thrown into a pond.<br>
All observable phenomena in our universe can be beautifully described through this single mechanism: the propagation of energy density waves in 4D space.<br>

### Abstract
This theory proposes a novel mathematical physics model that unifies all physical phenomena in the universe upon a static 4D block universe (Invariant Matrix) structure. It eliminates the standard assumption of "geometrical spacetime curvature" in General Relativity and dispenses with the need for extra dimensions.<br>
In this model, gravitational interactions and the curvature of light paths (gravitational lensing) are explained not as the deformation of space itself, but as the gradient $\nabla u$ of energy density $u$ on the 4D matrix and the associated refraction phenomena of waves.<br>
Furthermore, by integrating local "wave propagation (inertia)" and macroscopic "thermal equilibration (diffusion)" into a single unified equation, this framework consistently describes energy conservation (Noether's theorem) and cosmic circulation cycles with strict logical coherence.<br>
